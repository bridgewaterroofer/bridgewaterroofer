1. Roof Pitch & Complexity

Steep-slope roofs (8/12 pitch and greater) require specialized scaffolding, safety harnesses, and slower shingle placement, increasing labor hours compared to walkable 4/12 ranches.

2. Existing Roof Layers

New Jersey building codes permit a maximum of 2 roof layers. If your home has multiple existing layers, tear-off and disposal fees increase due to additional tonnage hauled.

3. Plywood Decking Condition

During tear-off, rotted or delaminated plywood sheathing must be replaced before installing underlayments. Plywood replacement typically averages $75 to $110 per 4x8 sheet installed.

Frequently Asked Roofing Cost & Payment Questions

How much does an architectural shingle replacement cost on an average Bridgewater home? +

For an average 2,200 to 2,600 sq ft home in Somerset County, a complete architectural tear-off and replacement with GAF Timberline HDZ or CertainTeed Landmark typically ranges from $10,500 to $15,800. This includes tear-off, synthetic underlayment, ice and water barrier along eaves and valleys, aluminum drip edge, ridge vent installation, municipal permits, and dumpster disposal.

Can I pay in monthly installments for an out-of-pocket roof replacement? +

Yes! We offer flexible self-pay financing options through leading home improvement lenders. Qualified homeowners can choose low monthly payments (starting around $149/mo) or 0% APR 12-month promotional plans. The application is paperless, takes minutes, and won't penalize you for early payoff.

What happens if rotten plywood decking is discovered during tear-off? +

Any damaged or soft CDX plywood sheathing is photographed and shown to you immediately. Replacement sheets are billed at our flat, pre-agreed rate ($75–$110 per sheet installed, including labor and fasteners). We never perform unapproved structural add-ons.

How do I get an exact price for my roof? +

Call us at (908) 465-9944 or request an estimate online. We perform a thorough on-site or high-resolution satellite roof assessment, inspect flashing and ventilation, and provide you with a written, guaranteed estimate outlining your material options and payment choices.

How do solar panels affect roof replacement costs? +

Homes with existing rooftop solar arrays require certified panel detachment and reinstallation during reroofing. Solar detach and reset services typically add $150 to $300 per panel (read our complete solar panel removal and roof replacement guide).

Should I replace skylights during a roof replacement to save money? +

Yes! Replacing aged skylights simultaneously with your roof replacement saves $800 to $1,500 in future labor and avoids tearing up brand-new shingles later (learn more in our guide on replacing skylights with a new roof and our skylight installation services).
