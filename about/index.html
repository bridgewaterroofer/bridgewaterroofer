WHO WE ARE

Somerset County's Trusted Local Roofing Craftsmen

Bridgewater Roofer is a full-service residential and commercial roofing contractor headquartered at 400 Commons Way in Bridgewater, NJ. Unlike transient storm chasers or regional franchise operations with rotating sub-contractors, our leadership and field crews live and raise our families right here in Somerset County.

We understand the unique microclimates and architectural demands of Central New Jersey: intense freeze-thaw expansion cycles, heavy snow accumulations in the Watchung Mountain corridors, dense oak and maple tree canopy moisture, and strict compliance with the New Jersey Uniform Construction Code (UCC).

Whether performing precision chimney step-flashing repairs on a Victorian home in Somerville, replacing architectural shingles on a colonial in Basking Ridge, or heat-welding a 60-mil TPO membrane on an office facility in Warren, we approach every project with engineering precision and uncompromising accountability.

OUR OPERATING VALUES

The Principles Guiding Every Roof We Build

We built our business on engineering discipline, straightforward pricing, and a total commitment to customer protection.

01

Honest Diagnostics

We never push full roof replacements when an isolated valley flashing repair or minor shingle replacement will safely restore weather integrity. Our estimates are factual, itemized, and transparent.

02

Complete Tear-Off Integrity

We never shingle over existing degraded layers. We strip roofs down to bare plywood or tongue-and-groove decking to identify hidden dry rot, re-nail loose boards, and apply dual ice and water barriers.

03

Factory-Certified Craftsmanship

Factory certification with GAF, CertainTeed, and Carlisle SynTec ensures that every roof is installed precisely to manufacturer specifications, qualifying our customers for 50-year non-prorated system warranties.
