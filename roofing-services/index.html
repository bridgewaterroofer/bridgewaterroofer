COMMON INQUIRIES

Frequently Asked Roofing Questions

Clear answers to help you make informed decisions for your home or commercial building.

How quickly can your crews respond to an emergency roof leak in Bridgewater? +

Because our operations headquarters are located at 400 Commons Way in Bridgewater, our emergency dispatch crews typically arrive on-site within 60 to 90 minutes. We deploy heavy-duty waterproof tarping and water containment to stop active ceiling leaks before scheduling permanent repairs.

How do I know if my roof needs a minor repair or a complete replacement? +

As a general rule, if your roof is under 15 years old and damage is confined to an isolated area (e.g. blown-off shingles or a compromised pipe boot), a targeted repair is recommended. However, if shingles are brittle, curling, missing heavy granules across multiple slopes, or approaching 20+ years of age, a full replacement provides far superior 50-year value and eliminates repeated emergency repair bills.

Do you provide written estimates before starting work? +

Yes. We provide completely transparent, itemized written estimates detailing the exact shingle brand, ice barrier underlayment, drip edge, ventilation upgrades, permit fees, and cleanup costs. Our pricing is 100% fixed with zero surprise add-ons.

Do you offer flexible monthly payment options for roof replacements? +

Yes! We offer direct homeowner financing plans including 0% APR promotional options and low monthly payment tiers starting around $149/month with no prepayment penalties. The application process is fast, paperless, and takes only minutes.

Do you handle township roofing permits in Bridgewater and Somerset County? +

Absolutely. In New Jersey, complete roof replacements require an official Uniform Construction Code (UCC) building permit. We manage the entire application with the Bridgewater Township Code Enforcement Department (or your local municipality) and schedule the final municipal inspection.
