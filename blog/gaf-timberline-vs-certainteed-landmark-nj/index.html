Head-to-Head Architectural Shingle Comparison

When replacing an asphalt roof in Bridgewater, Somerville, or Basking Ridge, 95% of homeowners select dimensional architectural shingles. Below is how the three industry leaders compare across critical New Jersey weather metrics:

Feature / Metric GAF Timberline HDZ CertainTeed Landmark Owens Corning Duration
Nailing Technology LayerLock (Massive Strike Zone) NailTrak (1.5" Guide Zone) SureNail (Embedded Fabric Band)
Wind Warranty 130 mph (Unlimited wind speed with 4 nails + GAF accessories) 110 mph standard (130 mph with 6 nails) 130 mph standard (4 nails)
Weight per Square ~215 lbs ~235 lbs (Heaviest mat) ~220 lbs
Algae Defense StainGuard Plus (25-Year Copper Microsite) StreakFighter (10-to-15 Year) StreakGuard (25-Year)
Installed Cost Index Moderate ($$) Premium ($$$) Moderate ($$)

1. GAF Timberline HDZ: The Wind & Speed Champion

GAF is North America's best-selling shingle brand, manufactured right here in New Jersey (headquartered in Parsippany). The flagship Timberline HDZ line introduced LayerLock Technology, which mechanically fuses the common bond layers together during manufacturing.

Why We Recommend It for Bridgewater: When installed with GAF starter shingles and ridge caps, GAF offers the WindProven™ Limited Wind Warranty with no maximum wind speed limitation—a major advantage for homes along exposed ridges in Martinsville or Warren facing coastal nor'easter gusts.

2. CertainTeed Landmark: The Heavyweight Classic

CertainTeed Landmark shingles are celebrated by purists for their substantial physical weight and heavy fiberglass base mat. At ~235 lbs per square, they are approximately 10% heavier than standard architectural shingles.

Aesthetics: Landmark's dual-layer design creates deep, rich shadow lines that replicate authentic wood cedar shake, making it a favorite for traditional colonial and center-hall homes in Bedminster, Basking Ridge, and Somerset County.

3. Owens Corning Duration: Patented SureNail Grip

Owens Corning's TruDefinition Duration stands out with its SureNail Technology—a tough, woven engineered fabric strip embedded directly in the nailing zone. This fabric band prevents nails from blowing through the fiberglass mat during rapid pneumatic gun fastening, delivering unmatched pull-through resistance.

Cost Per Square & Labor Transparency in Somerset County

On a typical single-family home in Bridgewater (2,500 square feet of roof surface, or 25 roofing squares):

  • GAF Timberline HDZ: Total turnkey installation generally ranges between $11,500 and $14,000 including full tear-off, synthetic felt, ice shield, drip edge, ridge vent, and lifetime warranties.
  • CertainTeed Landmark: Typically ranges between $12,200 and $15,000 reflecting higher material weight and freight costs.
  • Owens Corning Duration: Averages $11,800 to $14,200.

The Roofer's Verdict for Central New Jersey Homes

Which should you pick?

If your priority is unlimited wind warranty and algae defense under wooded canopy, choose GAF Timberline HDZ. If your priority is deep architectural shadow lines and heavier shingle mass on a high-pitch colonial, choose CertainTeed Landmark. Both deliver 30+ years of watertight protection when properly installed by master craftsmen.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I install architectural shingles over old 3-tab shingles? +

While New Jersey building code permits up to two layers of roofing, we strongly advise against a layover. Complete tear-off allows us to inspect structural plywood decking, replace rotted framing, and install modern self-adhering ice & water shield directly against the bare wood.

How long does an architectural shingle roof last in Bridgewater? +

With proper continuous intake and exhaust attic ventilation, a premium architectural roof in Central New Jersey lasts 25 to 35 years.
