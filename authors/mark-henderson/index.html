Professional Background & Expertise

Mark Henderson serves as Senior Roofing Consultant and Field Estimator for Bridgewater Roofer. Over the past nearly two decades, Mark has personally diagnosed and supervised more than 1,400 roof installations, historic restoration scopes, and storm remediation projects across Somerset County.

His deep familiarity with New Jersey Uniform Construction Code (UCC) requirements, regional microclimates along the Watchung Mountains, and strict manufacturer wind-uplift specifications ensures every Bridgewater Roofer project meets the highest engineering standards.
