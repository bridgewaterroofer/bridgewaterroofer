PREVENTATIVE AWARENESS

Small Roof Problems Can Become Larger Repairs

Roofing deterioration is cumulative. Understand how an isolated shingle compromise escalates over time when unaddressed.

01

Damaged Shingle

Wind cracks a shingle seal, hail loosens granules, or an old shingle tab lifts from temperature shock.

02

Moisture Entry

Wind-driven rain and snow melt migrate past the secondary felt barrier, seeking fastener penetrations.

03

Decking & Insulation

Plywood decking absorbs moisture, soft rot develops, and attic fiberglass insulation loses R-value.

04

Interior Water Damage

Ceiling rings, bubbling drywall, stained trim, and structural framing compromise emerge inside living areas.

DECISION GUIDE

Roof Repair or Roof Replacement?

The right option depends on the location, severity and overall condition of the roof system.

Roof Repair

Targeted Problem Correction

Best when the primary roofing material is still pliable, pliable seal strips remain functional, and damage is confined to a discrete area.

  • Localized damage from a fallen tree branch or high-wind gust
  • Isolated leaks around plumbing vent collars or chimney step flashing
  • A small grouping of missing shingles with sound surrounding field
  • Roofing is under 12–15 years old with good remaining service life
Explore Roof Repair

Roof Replacement

Complete System Modernization

Best when shingles are brittle, granule loss has exposed fiberglass mats, repeated leaks occur across planes, or age exceeds 20+ years.

  • Widespread deterioration, curling shingle edges, and brittle tabs
  • Multiple or recurring leaks showing up in different rooms
  • Existing roof already has 2 layers or has reached 20–25 years old
  • Desire for complete manufacturer warranty and energy-efficient ventilation

EDUCATIONAL PRICING GUIDE

What Affects Roofing Cost in Bridgewater?

Every roof has distinct architectural variables. We do not use misleading fixed prices. Understand the 8 factors that shape project estimates.

Roof Size 01

Total surface area measured in roofing "squares" (100 sq ft). Larger roof planes require more material and disposal volume.

Roof Pitch & Steepness 02

Steep pitches require specialized staging, roof jacks, safety harness lines, and slower, meticulous labor.

Roofing Material 03

Standard architectural shingles vs. high-definition designer shingles vs. standing seam metal systems.

Existing Layers 04

New Jersey code permits up to two layers. Removing multiple previous layers adds labor and landfill disposal fees.

Decking Condition 05

Hidden water rot may require replacing compromised plywood or tongue-and-groove boards before shingling.

Flashing & Ventilation 06

Chimney crickets, step flashing, skylight kits, pipe boots, and continuous ridge vent installation.

Property Access 07

Multi-story elevations, steep driveways, and delicate landscaping requiring protective plywood shields.

Project Complexity 08

Multiple intersecting valleys, dormers, turrets, and transitions increase detail cutting and waterproofing.
